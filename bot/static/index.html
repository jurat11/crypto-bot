Demo fills walk the live Binance order book and pay the account's taker fee. Slippage is in basis points (1 bp = 0.01%) against the mid price; positive means it cost money. For testnet fills, the line under the slippage shows what the simulator predicted from the same testnet order book, so real and simulated fills can be compared.

Balances and P&L are realized and marked-to-market numbers after fees: holdings are valued at the live mid price and every fee paid is already deducted. The only forward-looking numbers are the "what history says" ranges, taken from the backtest and labelled as such. Shorts in the long/short accounts are simulated. Past results do not predict future ones.
Stop all new trades?

Every account stops opening new trades within a minute. Closing trades, which cuts risk, still goes through. This creates a file named STOP in the bot folder; delete it to resume.
