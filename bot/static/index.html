Demo fills walk the live Binance order book and pay the 0.1% taker fee. Slippage is in basis points (1 bp = 0.01%) vs the mid price; positive means it cost money. For testnet fills, "real vs sim" compares the actual fill with what the simulator predicted from the same testnet book.
Balances and P&L are realized and marked-to-market numbers after fees: holdings are valued at the live mid price and every fee paid is already deducted. The only forward-looking numbers are the "what history says" ranges, taken from the backtest and labelled as such. Past results do not predict future ones.
Stop all new buys?
